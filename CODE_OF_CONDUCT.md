# Code of Conduct

> Language: English | [简体中文](zh-cn/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

SparkClaw contributors are expected to keep the project practical, respectful and safe for collaboration.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be clear about risks and tradeoffs.
- Keep feedback focused on code, documentation, safety and user outcomes.
- Credit prior work and external ideas when relevant.
- Handle private or sensitive information carefully.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Dismissing safety concerns without technical engagement.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion.
- Abusing project infrastructure or maintainers' time.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues, restrict participation or take other reasonable action to protect the project. If you experience or observe a problem, contact the maintainers privately when possible.
